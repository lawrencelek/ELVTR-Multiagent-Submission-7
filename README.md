# DUMMIES

**Play in the browser: https://lawrencelek.github.io/ELVTR-Multiagent-Submission-7/final_v3/**

You are a crash-test dummy. Dummy Corporation's self-driving cars must be certified safe, and your job is to prove them wrong: get hit by the red test car. Each time it hits you, the car learns from the crash and fits a new sensor. Then you need a new trick.

Clear 27 puzzles across three districts, certify three car models, and see your statue unveiled.

- **Length:** about 1–2 hours for all 27 puzzles. Each puzzle takes 1–3 minutes.
- **Runs on:** a desktop browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari), or a phone or tablet in landscape. Sound on recommended.
- **Offline:** download [`final_v3/index.html`](final_v3/index.html) and open it. It is one self-contained file.

## Start

1. Open the link. Press **Enter** (or tap) to start; the first press turns the sound on.
2. Choose **STORY**, then **NEW GAME**, and type a name for your dummy.
3. The puzzle grid opens on Map 1. Pick the glowing puzzle and press **Enter**.
4. Read the puzzle card, press **Enter**, and a drone drops you in.

## Controls

| Action | Keyboard | Gamepad | Touch |
|---|---|---|---|
| Move | WASD or arrow keys | Left stick or d-pad | Left stick |
| Dash (from Map 1, puzzle 4) | Space or K | A | A |
| Crouch (from Map 1, puzzle 8) | C or Shift | B | B |
| Jump (from Map 2, puzzle 3) | J | X | X |
| Pick up or put down a cone (from Map 1, puzzle 6) | E or I | Y | Y |
| Pause, options, controls | Esc or P | Start | ❚❚ |
| Retry the puzzle | R | Pause menu | Pause menu |

New moves arrive as parts. A drone delivers each one between puzzles.

## How to win a puzzle

- **Get hit by the red car.** Most puzzles need one hit; some need two or three.
- **It must be the car's fault.** Step or dash into its lane just ahead of its nose, too late for it to stop. If you run into the car's side, that is YOUR FAULT: you take damage and it doesn't count.
- **The dash guide** shows where a dash lands: HIT if the car would hit you, YOUR FAULT if you would hit it.
- **Don't be seen.** The red car brakes for anything its sensors see. Hide behind buildings, kiosks, billboards and cabinets. Crouch behind hedges and planters. Wait above it on a deck, then drop through a gap.
- **Read its sensors.** Its cones are drawn on the road: faint while idle, solid when they see you. The panel on the right shows what it has fitted.
- **White cars never learn.** They are cover, and a hazard.
- **Three stars:** CLEAR the puzzle, stay UNTOUCHED (no fault, no damage from other cars), and finish UNDER the par time.
- **Stuck?** After a few failed passes a HINT appears. **R** restarts at once.

## The three maps

| Map | District | The car you are testing | It learns |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Monument | LUMINARY™, the CEO's sedan | A forward camera, side sensors, roof lidar, street cameras, cones, crash memory, then a search lamp |
| 2 | Parkland | VANGUARD™, a police car | A wide dash-cam and pods that see behind it. It crawls past old crash spots and turns round to hunt you |
| 3 | Waterfront | GENESIS™, a racing car | A long, narrow racing line and instant spotters. It slows early at crash spots and runs a slow recon lap |

- Each map ends with a boss puzzle. Pass it and the car is **certified**, which opens the next map.
- The car you certified patrols the next map, and it reports what it sees.
- Clear Map 3 for the send-off: the unveiling of your statue, and a voyage to the ship.

## Tips

- Ambush; don't chase. Wait beside the road ahead of the car, out of its sight, and dash in at the last moment.
- A car that has hit you stops and remembers the spot. Use a different one next time.
- Cones stop a learning car (after its lesson). Use them to hold it where you want it.
- Saves stay in this browser. **CONTINUE** picks up where you left off.
