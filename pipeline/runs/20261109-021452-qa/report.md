## Analog

Off-axis input showed four things. First, stairs accept only a 0°/180° end-on entry; an approach 5-30° off pins on the hidden ramp side walls (C1-01 vs C1-06). Second, an 85° dash passes through the low hedge (B3-09). Third, 27°, 60° and 120° step-ins clear B1 like a 90° step does (B1-07, B1-08), so the forward camera has no angle weakness. Fourth, hide spots within 1-3 units of the kiosk corners block a straight 90° dash (B2-10); the pad's off-axis spring would be needed there, but it was not swept. No NaN or steering bend from a 720°/s spin during a dash (B2-07). Magnitudes of 0.1-0.3 at the lane edge were seen (B1-02, B3-07). The tests that would separate the pad from eight-way keys (dash angle sweeps of 60-80° from cover, dash length under partial stick) have not been run.
